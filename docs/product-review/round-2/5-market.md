# Theorie B: market & growth review (Sep 2026)

**Method note.** WebSearch worked, but WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy for every competitor domain (theorie.nl, itheorie.nl, theoriebaas.com, theorio.nl, cbr.nl, gratis-auto-theorie.nl). So the figures below come from search-result snippets and reseller pages, not from the vendors' own price pages. Cells marked *(est.)* are my own judgement or from memory and have **not been verified**. Treat prices as indicative: this market runs permanent "discounts".

**Context.** The CBR exam has been 50 questions, 30 min, pass mark 44 since 7 Apr 2025, and costs €50.50 in 2026. The first-time pass rate is about 40% (39.7% in 2024, 40% in 2025) ([theorie.nl](https://www.theorie.nl/examenvoorbereiding/auto-theorie-examen-b/slagingspercentage-theorie-auto), [theoriesnelhalen](https://www.theoriesnelhalen.nl/nieuws-blogs/cbr-tarieven-2026-prijzen-theorie-examen)). A failed attempt costs about €50 plus weeks of waiting, so paying €30–50 for prep is easy to justify. That is why the market is crowded and SEO-saturated.

## 2. Where it can win and where it cannot

**It can win on:**
- **Trust and verifiability.** It is the only product that cites the law article behind each answer. Teachers, NT2 coaches and careful learners care about this. Competitors make vague "officiële CBR-vragen" claims, which this app can contrast with openly.
- **Zero friction.** No account, no paywall, works offline. It fits schools and community organisations that can't make pupils sign up, and budget-constrained learners (students, newcomers, people on benefits).
- **Revision and deliberate practice.** Spaced repetition plus a mistake log that only clears after two correct days is better learning design than "unlimited exams".
- **Its niche.** Retake candidates who failed on *knowledge*, not hazard perception, and learners who use it next to a paid course.

**It cannot compete on:**
- **Hazard perception, photos and video.** This is a large, costly part of the exam, and CBR-style photos are licensed or expensive to shoot.
- **Question volume** (200 vs 1,500–3,500).
- **SEO head terms.** "theorie examen oefenen" and "gratis auto theorie" are owned by dozens of exact-match domains.
- **Paid acquisition and bundled day courses.**

The honest message is: *use this as your free second tool, not your only one.*

## 3. Positioning

> For Dutch B-licence learners who want to *understand* the rules and not memorise answers, Theorie B is the free, ad-free, account-free practice app where every answer shows the law it comes from, so you know it is right and why.

Taglines:
1. **"Oefenen met bewijs: bij elk antwoord het wetsartikel."**
2. **"Gratis. Geen account. Geen reclame. Wel de regels."**
3. **"Snap de regel, haal je theorie."**

## 4. Zero-budget growth plan

- **SEO on long-tail rule pages, not exam pages.** Generate one static, indexable page per rule or question cluster from the existing data: "voorrang van rechts uitzondering", "RVV artikel 15 uitleg", "promillage beginnend bestuurder", "bord C2 betekenis", "hoe hard binnen de bebouwde kom aanhanger". Each page should cite the article and link into the app. Low-competition queries like these are where legal citations earn links. Also add a "Regels in getallen" cheat-sheet page, which people share and bookmark.
- **Rijscholen.** Offer a free "huiswerk-link" and a printable QR poster, pitched as "free revision for your pupils; we don't sell courses". Many small schools resell iTheorie and similar products, so position this as a complement, not a replacement. Target independent instructors on Facebook groups and regional instructor associations.
- **VO/MBO.** Many pupils take the theory exam at 16½–17. Pitch mentors and "burgerschap" or verkeer modules. Account-free use is AVG-friendly, which is a real selling point for schools.
- **NT2 / newcomers.** Taalhuizen, VluchtelingenWerk, municipal inburgering (Wet inburgering 2021) and libraries. Many newcomers need a licence for work. The CBR interpreter exam costs about €350 ([theorie.nl](https://www.theorie.nl/examenvoorbereiding/auto-theorie-examen-b/theorie-examen-met-tolk)), so a clear-Dutch or English app has real value. Needs: a glossary and plain-language mode (B1).
- **Reddit.** Answer threads helpfully on r/Netherlands, r/thenetherlands and r/expats (theory-exam threads come up weekly *(est.)*). Open source is credible there.
- **TikTok/Reels.** Series such as "Deze voorrangsvraag zakt 6 op 10", using the top-down intersection animations with a law-article reveal. Cost is zero, the format fits naturally, and the national 40% pass rate is the hook.
- **Open-source channels.** GitHub topic tags, a Show HN / Tweakers "Meuktracker" post, and a listing on the Dutch "digitale gemeenschapsgoederen" / Public Code lists.

## 5. Sustainability without ads

- **Stay free by keeping costs near €0.** Static hosting (GitHub Pages) means the real cost is authoring time.
- **Institutional sponsorship.** Candidates include VVN (Veilig Verkeer Nederland), provincial road-safety budgets (ROV), SIDN Fonds, municipalities (inburgering) and libraries. Fund content such as photos, translations and hazard clips as public goods under an open licence.
- **Donations** via "Betaal wat je wilt na je geslaagde examen", with a Tikkie/iDEAL link on the pass screen.
- **Fair paid tier (optional):** a one-off €5–9, no subscription, for *extras* only: hazard-perception clip packs, extra mock-exam sets, printable PDF. The core stays free forever. A B2B "klas-dashboard" for schools and rijscholen could cost about €50/yr, but only if privacy stays local-first.
- **Avoid** fake "garantie" claims and selling data. Both would destroy the trust position.

## 6. Top 5 product bets (by expected adoption impact)

1. **Hazard-perception practice.** Even 30 photo or illustrated "gas los / remmen / niets" scenarios would close the biggest credibility gap. Build them as SVG scenes (cheap) before any video.
2. **Question bank to 600+ with photo-realistic situations.** Volume is the first thing users compare.
3. **Indexable static rule pages generated from the data** (SEO engine, see §4). This is the distribution channel.
4. **English and plain-Dutch (B1) mode plus a glossary.** This opens the expat/NT2 niche, which Theorio currently owns on a paid basis.
5. **Shareable teacher/rijschool links and a readiness indicator** ("klaar voor examen?" based on the last 3 mock exams and open mistakes). This drives referrals and gives learners a reason to return.
